三和弦、转位与 CAGED：指板乐理的骨架

只会按开放大横按远远不够。把三和弦拆成 1–3–5 三个音，在 1-2-3 / 2-3-4 / 3-4-5 三组弦上练熟三个转位，再用 CAGED 把同一个和弦首尾相接铺满整条琴颈，用声部连接让伴奏像唱片一样顺滑。

学完能做到

2. 逐个概念

① 四种三和弦的构造与听感

是什么
三和弦由两个三度叠成：大三 = M3+m3（1 3 5，明亮稳定）；小三 = m3+M3（1 ♭3 5，暗沉柔情）；减三 = m3+m3（1 ♭3 ♭5，极度紧张，大调 vii°）；增三 = M3+M3（1 3 ♯5，对称悬空）。
为什么需要
一切七和弦与扩展和弦都是在这四个核心三和弦底座上往上叠加音符。
在指板上
在任意一个大三按法里找到三音，往低品降 1 品就变小三；再降五音变减三；只把大三的五音升高 1 品就变增三。
听起来
常见误解
“和弦是死背的按法表。”记住 1-3-5 在手型里的位置，一个按法就能衍生成四个和弦。

② 转位（Inversion）与 1-2-3 / 2-3-4 三弦组

是什么
谁在最低音决定转位：根音在低音叫原位；三音在低音叫第一转位（写作 C/E）；五音在低音叫第二转位（写作 C/G）。
为什么需要
乐队合奏或录音时，大横按 6 根弦全扫往往与贝斯、键盘打架。把三和弦拆成只弹相邻 3 根弦（1-2-3 或 2-3-4 弦）是电吉他节奏与 Funk/Pop 的核心武器。
在指板上
2-3-4 弦上的 C 大三三个转位：5-5-5（G-C-E 第二转位）→ 10-9-8（C-E-G 原位）→ 14-12-13（E-G-C 第一转位）。
听起来
常见误解
“C/E 是 C 和弦加上低音 E 的附加音。”斜线右边的 E 就是 C 和弦原本的 3 音，所以这是 C 的第一转位，不是新加了外音。

③ CAGED 五指型逻辑（JustinGuitar）

是什么
五个开放大三指型 C → A → G → E → D 当成可移动的横按夹子，沿琴颈从低品到高品依次首尾相接，拼出同一个和弦的 5 个把位。
为什么需要
后面 G10 五声音阶 5 个把位和 G13 琶音全指板网络，全都直接挂在这 5 个 CAGED 骨架上。
在指板上
以 C 和弦为例：开放 C(0-3品) 用 5 弦 3 品 C 接 A 型(3-5品) → A 型 3/1 弦 5 品与 6 弦 8 品接 G 型(5-8品) → E 型(8-10品) → D 型(10-13品)。在下方实验室依次点击 C/A/G/E/D 查看！
听起来
常见误解
“5 个 CAGED 指型都要 6 根弦死按大横按。”实战中 G 型和 C 型高把位几乎不按满 6 根弦，都是截取其中 3～4 根弦的片段来弹。

④ 声部连接（Voice Leading）：就近移动的魔力

是什么
从一个和弦换到下一个和弦时，尽量让共同音保持在原位不动，其余声部只做半音或全音的级进移动。
为什么需要
全指板大跳大横按听起来生硬且节奏容易断；就近转位听起来则像几条流畅的小旋律线同时在流动。
在指板上
2-3-4 弦弹 C–F–G：C(5-5-5: G-C-E) → 保持 3 弦 5 品 C 不动，变 F(7-5-6: A-C-F) → 整体上移 2 品变 G(9-7-8: B-D-G)！
听起来
常见误解
“换和弦就是手掌跳去下一个根音。”贝斯手已经管住低音根音了，电吉他手在中高音弦走最短声部距离反而最高级。

⑤ 五度强力和弦（Power Chord）

是什么
只包含根音 + 纯五度（1 5，常叠加高八度 1），完全省略三音的二音和弦（如 E5、A5）。
为什么需要
开高增益过载/失真时，放大器会产生大量和频与差频（互调失真）。纯五度频率比是干净的 3:2，失真下不浑浊；大小三度比例较复杂，重失真下容易糊成一团。
在指板上
6 弦或 5 弦根音，下一根弦 +2 品就是五音；跨 3–2 弦时需要 +3 品（记住 3–2 弦规律！）。
听起来
常见误解
“强力和弦也是大三和弦。”它没有三音，所以既不是大也不是小，在大小调与各种调式 Riff 里都能通吃。

4. 上琴练习

  1. 三组弦三个转位（60 BPM）：在 1-2-3 弦、2-3-4 弦上分别弹出 C、F、G 的原位、第一转位、第二转位，边弹边报 1/3/5。重点验证：按节拍稳定、说得出级数与音名。
  2. CAGED 五把位串联（70 BPM）：选 C 和 A 两个和弦，从开放把位顺 C→A→G→E→D 弹到 12 品并原路弹回。重点验证：按节拍稳定、说得出级数与音名。
  3. 三音降半音变小三（不看图）：5 个 CAGED 大三指型全部改造成小三（困难的 G/C 型截取 3～4 根弦）。重点验证：按节拍稳定、说得出级数与音名。
  4. 4 品格内 I–IV–V 声部连接（80 BPM 伴奏）：只用 2-3-4 弦在 4 个品位内走完 C–F–G（以及换到 D 调 D–G–A），录下来回听。重点验证：按节拍稳定、说得出级数与音名。

Triads三和弦

Key Takeaways要点
  • A triad is a three-note chord whose notes can be arranged in thirds. A triad can always be "stacked" so that its notes are either on all lines or all spaces.三和弦是由三个可以按三度叠置排列的音构成的和弦。在五线谱上缩成最紧凑形式时，它的三个音要么全在线上、要么全在间里。
  • When a triad is stacked in its most compact form in thirds, the lowest note is called the root, the middle note is called the third, and the highest note is called the fifth.当三和弦以最紧凑的三度形式叠放时，最下面的音叫根音，中间叫三音，最上面叫五音。
  • There are four qualities of triad. A major triad's third is major and its fifth is perfect, while a minor triad's third is minor and its fifth is perfect. A diminished triad's third is minor and its fifth is diminished, while an augmented triad's third is major and its fifth is augmented.三和弦共有四种性质：大三和弦（大三度 + 纯五度）、小三和弦（小三度 + 纯五度）、减三和弦（小三度 + 减五度）与增三和弦（大三度 + 增五度）。
  • In chord symbols, major triads are represented with capital letters that correspond to the triad's root. Minor triads have a lowercase "mi" after the letter, diminished triads have a superscript circle (o), and augmented triads have a plus sign (+). You may see other abbreviations in other contexts (a more exhaustive list is given in the Chord Symbols chapter).在和弦符号中：大三只写大写根音字母；小三在字母后加小写“mi”；减三右上角加圆圈（o）；增三加加号（+）。其他流行写法详见和弦符号一章。
  • Within major and minor keys, triads have particular qualities that correspond to scale degree. These are the same in every major and minor key, which makes memorizing them useful.在大小调体系里，音阶各级上搭起来的三和弦性质固定不变，背下这套固定模式极有帮助。
  • Triads are identified by their root, quality, and inversion.分析三和弦永远围绕三个维度：根音、性质和转位。

Triads三和弦构造

The three notes of a triad can always be arranged in thirds. Example 1 shows two triads, each written both melodically and harmonically. The first triad is on three adjacent spaces, while the second triad is on three adjacent lines. A triad can always be "stacked" so that its notes are either on all lines or all spaces. When a triad is stacked in its most compact form (measures 2 and 4 of Example 1), it looks like a snowperson (see Example 2). Just as a snowperson consists of a bottom, middle, and head, a triad consists of lowest, middle, and upper notes.任何三和弦的三个音都可以排成两个紧挨着的三度。例 1 用旋律分解形式和和声柱式形式分别写了两个三和弦（前一个是连着三个间，后一个是连着三条线）。当三和弦叠得最紧凑（第 2、4 小节）时，长相就像一个三段小雪人（图例 2）——有底球、中球和脑袋。

Triadic Qualities and Listening to Triads四种三和弦性质与听辨

There are four qualities of triad—major, minor, diminished, and augmented—which are determined by the quality of the intervals from the root to the third and the root to the fifth. These qualities are labeled in Example 4: triad qualities are shown above the staff, the quality of the fifth to the root is given in red to the left of the triad, and the quality of the third is given in blue to the right of the triad. Augmented triads are shown last because the other three types are more common in most classical and popular music.三和弦有大、小、减、增四种性质，完全由“根音→三音”和“根音→五音”这两个音程决定。例 4 在谱表上方标出了名称，左侧红色写出五度性质，右侧蓝色写出三度性质（增三放在最后，因为前三种在各类音乐里更常用）。

Note that triads are named for certain important intervals they contain:留意三和弦是怎么凭关键音程定名字的：

  • Major and minor triads are named for the quality of their third. (Both have perfect fifths.)大三与小三和弦：它们的五度都是纯五度，所以按“三音”的大小命名。
  • Diminished and augmented triads are named for the quality of their fifth:减三与增三和弦：按它们独特的“五音”命名：
    • A diminished triad's fifth is diminished. (Its third is minor.)减三和弦的五音是减五度（配小三度）。
    • An augmented triad's fifth is augmented. (Its third is major.)增三和弦的五音是增五度（配大三度）。

Listen carefully to the different qualities of triad in Example 4. It is common to pair expressive qualities with triads when learning what they sound like. You might think of major triads as sounding "happy," minor triads as "sad," diminished triads as "scary," and augmented triads as having a "fantasy" or "mystical" sound.仔细听例 4 的音响色彩区别：初学者常把大三听成“明亮/快乐”，小三听成“忧郁/悲伤”，减三像“紧张/悬疑”，增三则带着一股“奇幻/飘渺”的味道。

Chord Symbols三和弦标记符号

Chord symbols for triads include the letter name of the root and an indication of the triad’s quality, and sometimes the pitch class of the bass voice (meaning the lowest note in the chord, not any particular instrument or voice type).完整的三和弦和弦符号包含根音字母、性质缩写，有时后面还带一个斜杠标明最低音（bass，指整个多声部中最低的那个实际音高，不专指贝斯乐器）。

A chord symbol begins with a capital letter (and, if necessary, an accidental) denoting the root of the chord. That letter is followed by information about a chord’s quality. Open Music Theory will use the following abbreviations.[1]符号最开头是一个大写字母（需要时带升降号）代表根音，随后用缩写标出性质。Open Music Theory 统一采用下列记法：[1]

  • Major triad: no quality symbol is added大三和弦：根音字母后面什么都不加
  • Minor triad: lowercase “mi”小三和弦：后缀小写“mi”
  • Diminished triad: superscript circle (o)减三和弦：右上角圆圈（o）
  • Augmented triad: plus sign (+)增三和弦：后缀加号（+）

For example, the chord symbols C, Cmi, Co, and C+ mean a C major triad, C minor triad, C diminished triad, and C augmented triad, respectively. If the root of the chord has an accidental, include it: for example, B♭mi is the chord symbol for a B♭ minor triad, and F♯o is the chord symbol for an F♯ diminished triad.比如 C、Cmi、Co、C+ 分别代表 C 大三、C 小三、C 减三和 C 增三；根音带升降时把升降号紧跟字母，如 B♭mi、F♯o。

Finally, if a pitch class other than the chord root is the lowest note in the chord, a slash is added, followed by a capital letter denoting the pitch class in the bass. This topic will be explored more in the chapter titled Inversion. Example 5 first shows C major and C minor triads with their chord symbols (C and Cmi). It then shows how the chord symbol for the C minor triad changes when a note other than C is on the bottom: when E♭ is the lowest note, the chord symbol is Cmi/E♭, and when G is the lowest note, the symbol is Cmi/G.最后，当最低音不是根音时，用斜杠加上最低音字母（详情见 转位 一章）。例 5 先演示原位的 C 与 Cmi，然后展示同样是 C 小三和弦：一旦最低音变成 E♭ 就记成 Cmi/E♭，最低音变成 G 就记成 Cmi/G。

Triad Qualities in Major and Minor大小调中的顺阶三和弦性质

Any note of the major scale can be the root of a triad. As you can see in Example 6, which is in the key of G major, triads built on do, fa, and sol (1̂, 4̂, and 5̂) in major keys are major, shown with the capital letter of the triad’s root. Triads built on re, mi, and la (2̂, 3̂, and 6̂) are minor, shown with a lowercase “mi” after the capital letter of the root. Triads built on ti (7̂) are diminished; this is shown with a superscript "o" (which you might know as the degree symbol). These triadic qualities do not change in different keys; in other words, the quality of a triad built on do (1̂) will always be major in any major key.大调音阶任意一音都可以作根音叠起三和弦。以例 6（G 大调）为例：建在 do, fa, sol（1̂, 4̂, 5̂）上的永远是大三和弦；建在 re, mi, la（2̂, 3̂, 6̂）上的永远是小三和弦；建在 ti（7̂）上的是减三和弦。这套性质分布规律在任何 12 个大调里都完全一样——只要记住它，换什么调都一秒推导。

Triadic Inversion三和弦转位

Musicians often prioritize the note that is in the bass voice of triadic harmonies, simply called the "bass," which is the lowest part (or voice) of a composition, regardless of what instrument or voice type is singing or playing that lowest note. Example 1. An A major triad in root position, first inversion, and second inversion.例 1. 原位、第一转位与第二转位的 A 大三和弦。

An A major triad consists of three notes, the root (A), the third (C♯), and the fifth (E). When a triad is stacked in thirds (i.e. "snowperson form"), we say the triad is in root position. The bass note in root position is the root. Chords that do not have the root in the bass are said to be inverted. As you can see in Example 1, when the third is in the bass the chord is in first inversion and when the fifth is in the bass we say the chord is in second inversion. Chord symbols followed by a slash and a letter name indicate the pitch which is in the bass.一个 A 大三和弦总是由 A（根）、C♯（三）和 E（五）组成。用雪人三度原位叠加且低音弹根音 A 就是原位；一旦最低音不再是根音，和弦就叫做转位了（inverted）。三音 C♯ 当最低音叫做第一转位（标记 A/C♯）；五音 E 当最低音叫做第二转位（标记 A/E）。

It is important to note that the bass voice of the chord is NOT the same thing as the chord's root. The root of an A major triad is always A, regardless of whether the triad is in root position, first inversion, or second inversion. However, the bass voice changes between these inversions, from A to C♯ to E, as seen in Example 1.**千万不要把“低音”和“根音”搞混！**不管 A 大三和弦变成第几转位、贝斯手在下面弹 A 还是 C♯ 还是 E，这个和弦的根音永远都是 A，变化的仅仅是最低音。

7. 自测 5 题

1. E 型大横按（如 5 品 A）中哪根弦是 3 音？变小三和弦要动哪根手指？
答案

3 弦 6 品（C♯）是 3 音；抬起中指退回食指横按的 5 品（C），就变成 Am。

2. C 型和 A 型首尾相连时，共用的是哪个音？
答案

5 弦上的根音（例如 C 型 5 弦 3 品 C，直接变成 A 型横按的起点根音）。

3. 为什么说增三和弦在十二平均律里一共只有 4 个不同的音集？
答案

因为每隔 4 个半音（大三度）循环一次，12÷4=3 个音对称，C+、E+、G♯+ 完全是同一组音的转位！

4. D/F♯ 和 D/C 各是什么性质的斜线和弦？
答案

D/F♯ 是 D 大三和弦的第一转位（3 音 F♯ 在低音）；D/C 是低音 C（♭7）上叠 D 三和弦的复合声响。

5. 上琴题：在 2-3-4 弦上弹出 D–G–A（I–IV–V），保证移动幅度不超过 3 个品位。
答案

D(7-7-7) → G(9-7-8) → A(11-9-10 或 7-6-5)，共同音与半全音连接一气呵成。
